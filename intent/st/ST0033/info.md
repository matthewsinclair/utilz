# ST0033: A test surface for arngz's engine, starting with exec_create_space

## Objective

Give arngz's engine a proof surface where reading is the only proof there is, starting with `exec_create_space`, the one function this project has measured as beyond reading.

The surface injects a fake `hs` so the engine's own code runs under plain `lua`, with no Hammerspoon, no pointer, no Mac and no desk booking, and sits inside `utilz test` and CI beside `plan_test.lua`. Its first delivery is a driver that reaches every reporting path of `create_space`, including the two a live run cannot produce to order, with every arm proven by a mutation that makes it go red.

The scope is one function, deliberately. `exec_place` was measured as not needing this, and widening it to the whole engine is the over-correction this thread's own evidence exists to prevent.

## Context

ST0032 applied one standard, "the engine's coordination is small enough to read in the bytes", to two engine functions on the same day, by the same author, in the same words. It held for `exec_place`, which had four readings by three readers with no defect found, and failed three times for `exec_create_space`, which took four commits with a defect found after each one, every one by a reader rather than by the reading.

The distinction that predicts it is state, an effect, and an order between them. `exec_place` is a stateless mapping and has a genuine live proof, since `acceptance.sh`'s apply mode drives it every run. `exec_create_space` has a retry loop, two captured mutable locals, run-scoped state outliving the call, and an ordering dependency between an effect that creates and a read known to lag. It has no suite coverage at all, `create_space` appearing in `acceptance.sh` zero times, and issue 0135's design claimed a live proof citing a line inside the observe verb that could not touch a space creation.

What the arms must assert follows from utilz-vc's distillation: the arithmetic was never wrong, and what was wrong every time was the function's own account of what it had done. An arm checking only that a space was made would have passed on every defective version.

hv ruled twice on 10 Nov 2026, first-hand in dc's window: "Hold until C5B9WJ is green, don't ship it unrun", and then, on being told the arms' green cannot reach this function at all, "write the driver for exec_create_space before the cut". So 2.14.9's cut waits on this thread. Recorded as utilz-vc's decisions 96 and 97; ST0032 is related and carries the measurement.
