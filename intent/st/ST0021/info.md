# ST0021: showreel exports a reel as a video file

## Objective

`showreel video <dir>` writes a reel as a video file, `.mp4` or `.mov`, whose frames are exactly what the HTML reel shows at each moment. So a reel can play where a browser cannot. hv wants it in 2.10.0, and the release waits for it (hv's board, decisions 5 and 6).

## Context

hv, 2026-09-19: "I also want to add a new feature to showreel, which is the ability to generate a .mov of .mp4 (ie video file) of the showreel. More to come, but I want that capability in the 2.10.0 release." Asked how far away it is, hv answered that they are open to every route and dependency the design puts forward (decision 6).

showreel's `check` and `build` are already Rust (ST0017, `crates/showreel/src/main.rs:31` and `:38`). Only `init` and `qr` remain in the Python prototype. A video export reads what `build` reads, so it needs neither of them ported first.

`design.md` carries the route, the spike that sized it, the questions for hv and the work packages. cc drafts it, vc reviews it before any code, and vc takes the questions to hv.
