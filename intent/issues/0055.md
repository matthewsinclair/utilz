`--safe-zone social` insets the LAYOUT: every pane's padding, the marks and the caps are measured from the zone's box (ST0024 D5c). A theme that paints OUTSIDE an element's layout box therefore puts ink in the zone's bands, and nothing in the design can see it: the zone works in CSS lengths, and the fit measures glyph metrics from a canvas, which exclude a `text-shadow` and a `box-shadow` by definition.

MEASURED 2026-09-22 by vc, reading ST0024's AC-02.5 on the real reel rather than on a fixture: `20260919-45h-forbiddenplanet-nottingham-001`, the popupart theme, recorded at 9:16 with and without `--safe-zone social`, 1911 frames each.

- **socials page** (flat blue ground, so a not-background count is exact): the top, right and bottom bands hold ZERO non-background pixels. The left band holds 293, in a bounding box of 8x106 at x=57..64, hard against the zone's left edge at 65, on the headline's rows alone. The zone HAS moved the page inward -- in the unzoned recording the same band carries the wordmark at x=16..64.
- **strapline page**: the three button blocks and their hard shadows cross the zone's right edge, by about 19 px of 1080.
- The cause is the theme: `themes/popupart/theme.css` paints `text-shadow: 8px 8px 0` and `12px 12px 0`, strokes, and rotates its display type. `.socials h2` in the player declares no shadow at all.

WHAT IS NOT WRONG: nothing is cut, truncated or obscured. Every line and every mark of the reel sits inside the zone, and the pages are legible at phone width (read at 390 px). The overhang is decorative ink, 0.7% of the frame's width on the left and under 2% on the right, and the platform's own UI at those edges is drawn over a band far wider than the overhang.

WHAT IS WRONG IS A CLAIM: AC-02.2 says "no type or mark has ink in the zone's four bands", and `video.sh`'s ST0024-AT06 proves exactly that -- on ITS OWN fixture, with the built-in theme, whose type paints inside its box. The green is true and narrower than the sentence, which is the same shape as issue 0054.

THE CHOICES, and this is hv's rather than a defect to fix quietly: (a) leave it, and narrow AC-02.2 and AT06's wording to the layout box, which is what the zone controls; (b) widen the zone's insets by a theme-independent margin, which penalises every theme for one theme's shadows; (c) have the zone read each fitted element's painted bounds (`getBoundingClientRect` does not include shadows either, so this needs a real design). vc recommends (a) for 2.12.0, with this issue as the record.

## Resolution

hv, 2026-09-22, asked in both sessions: "Narrow". Option (a), for 2.12.0, and the zone does not move.

`AC-02.2` now says every mark and line of type has its LAYOUT BOX inside the zone, names shadow, stroke and rotation as paint the zone does not inset, and cites this issue. `video.sh`'s ST0024-AT06 carries the same in a comment above the check and its title now reads "nothing of type or mark is LAID OUT in the platform's four bands", so the green and the sentence a reader takes from it are the same size. The criterion and this issue point at each other.

The general form, which devbin has taken into its lessons draft: **the instrument reported LAYOUT extent and the claim was about PAINTED extent.** The two words differ, and the substitution stayed invisible because they agree on the common case -- which is the same shape as issue 0054, measured the same day.
