## What

hv, 18 Oct 2026, looking at menuz's Desks… flyout: "when I choose "Discover" the default shouldn't name be "today" it should be the name of the currently selected desk. And that also implies that there is a "currently selected desk". So we should put a tick mark here", beside that desk in the flyout.

arngz has no notion of a current desk, so neither half can be done. menuz's half (a default the shell fills in, and a tick) is its own issue.

## Measured

18 Oct 2026, at `2f46784`. arngz keeps no state of its own.

- `cmd_apply` (`opt/arngz/arngz:1325`) runs the engine, prints its report and exits with the engine's 0 or 1.
- `cmd_discover --save` (`:1428`) writes the desk's file into the catalogue.
- `cmd_delete` (`:1129`) removes one.
- Nothing under `opt/arngz` reads or writes `XDG_STATE_HOME`.

So nothing can say which desk the screen was last arranged as.

## Fix

Ruled 18 Oct 2026, in hv's stead:

1. An `apply` that ran records the desk's name in `${XDG_STATE_HOME:-~/.local/state}/arngz/current`. That is exit 0 or 1, and neither `--dry-run` nor a refusal before anything changed. A `discover --save <desk>` records it too, since the arrangement on screen is then that desk.
2. A `delete` of the recorded desk clears the record.
3. A new verb, `arngz current`, prints the recorded name and exits 0. It exits 1 when nothing is recorded, or when the recorded desk is no longer in the catalogue.
4. `list` prints what it prints today.

`help/arngz.md` and the CHANGELOG describe it. arngz goes from 1.0.1 to 1.1.0.
