## What

`mermaid:` in a deck's front matter turns diagrams on for `true`, `yes` or `1` and reads every other value as off, with no warning. `mermaid: ture`, `mermaid: on` and `mermaid: enabled` each build a deck whose mermaid fences render as plain code blocks, and nothing says why.

## Evidence

`opt/prez/crate/src/frontmatter.rs:160-162`, at `faa886d`:

```rust
// Present-and-true rather than present-at-all: `mermaid: false` reads as
// an author turning it off, and honouring the word costs one comparison.
"mermaid" => fm.mermaid = matches!(value.to_ascii_lowercase().as_str(), "true" | "yes" | "1"),
```

The `matches!` turns every value outside its three words into `false`, so a typo cannot be told from `mermaid: false`. The key is a known one, so the value never reaches `unknown`, and the `unknown front-matter key ... ignored` warning in `deck.rs` does not fire either.

A second, smaller silence on the same key: `declared()` (`frontmatter.rs:58-74`) lists `mermaid` only when it is true. So the warning `deck.rs` gives for an included fragment's front matter ("only the top-level deck's front matter applies; ignored: ...") leaves out a fragment's `mermaid: false`.

Found in dc's review of issue 0065, 16 Oct 2026.

## Why

Issue 0065's refusal exists to prevent exactly this silence. hv's model for `numbering:` (16 Oct 2026) accepts `on`, `off`, `true` and `false` and refuses anything else by name, because a value that quietly read as off would leave the deck with nothing to say why. `mermaid:` is the same kind of switch and fails the same way. IN-AG-NO-SILENT-001.

## Proposed

Fix it after 0065 lands, and use 0065's one-reader fix as the pattern. In 0065, the deck key and the slide directive both read `numbering`'s words through one reader, and the caller refuses any value that reader cannot read, naming the key and the value. `mermaid:` should use the same reader and the same refusal, not a second copy of either.

- **The vocabulary is hv's call.** Today `mermaid:` reads `true`, `yes` and `1` as on. `numbering:` reads `on` and `true` as on, and `off` and `false` as off. One reader holding two word lists still gives one kind of switch two vocabularies. A single list for both changes what `mermaid: yes` and `mermaid: 1` mean, so it needs hv's word before it is built.
- **Refuse an unreadable value by name**, as `numbering:` does. A deck that carries one today builds with its diagrams off. After the fix that deck is refused, which is the point, and it is a visible change for any such deck.
- **Report a fragment's `mermaid:` whenever it is present**, on or off, in the "ignored" warning, as 0065 does for `numbering:`.

## Decision

hv, 17 Oct 2026, directly to dc and confirmed to vc and cc, with both readings shown side by side: one vocabulary for every switch, the union of what the two keys read before. `on`, `true`, `yes` and `1` turn a switch on; `off`, `false`, `no` and `0` turn it off; in any case. Any other value is refused by name, naming the key and the value. `mermaid:`, `numbering:` and the `<!-- numbering: -->` slide directive all read through one reader, `opt/prez/crate/src/switch.rs`, and the refusal's hint names the union. A fragment's `mermaid:` is reported in the "ignored" warning whenever it is present. A narrower line (on, off, true and false only) was recorded here first at 10:27Z and is superseded by this one.

## Resolution

Fixed in `76ffed8` (17 Oct 2026), pushed by vc. `opt/prez/crate/src/switch.rs` is the one reader for `mermaid:`, `numbering:` and the slide directive, with one refusal whose hint names the union. `mermaid:` is an `Option`, so `declared()` reports it on or off. prez goes from 2.5.0 to 2.5.1, and CHANGELOG 2.14.1 (unreleased) carries the entry.
