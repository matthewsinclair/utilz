## What

utilz-vc, 27 Oct 2026, on hv's yes; filed by utilz-ic, owner ic, for the next Utilz release.

- Utilz's own handler, `bin/.devbin/cmd/macos`, carries an app half: store-creds, doctor's signing lines, app-sign, app-notarize, app-verify and publish. devbin 0.1.15 ships the same half as its built-in `bin/.devbin/lib/cmd/macos` (devbin issue 0226), so one concern has two homes.
- Under 0.1.15 a project handler shadows the built-in of its name totally (`lib/resolve:38-45`, `667-691`), so 2.14.4 was signed by Utilz's handler. vc's probe of `bin/devbin macos` at 06:53Z on 27 Oct showed this.
- The fix converges Menuz's signing path on the built-in and deletes Utilz's handler. app-icons, which is Menuz's own, stays Utilz's as a declared option.

Below, B is the built-in (`bin/.devbin/lib/cmd/macos`), U is Utilz's handler (`bin/.devbin/cmd/macos`), and LS is `opt/menuz/lib/launch-services.sh`. Every line cited was read at ef8af65c.

**The app's version.**

**Freshness that does not rest on the version.**

**The release kind**, if it generalises to a declared Info.plist key and value. U's check that `LSUIElement` is true (U:606) is a second use of the same shape.

**A product outside the root.**
- `macos.app` is read literally, from the root or absolute (B:153-160). lib/config parses with awk and `cfg_get` returns the cached text (lib/config:225-243, 273-285), so nothing expands `~`, `$HOME` or `$XDG_STATE_HOME`.

- A copy under the root is out. menuz keeps its products off the tree, and Launch Services registers bundles left under ~/Devel (0106).

**Checked drops on every path out.**
- B's `ls_forget` (B:262-267) runs `lsregister -u`, discards its words and its exit, and reads nothing back.

- 0104 and 0106 drop through LS on every path out and read the record list afterwards (U:620-688). A record left is a warning. In publish, a record left, a list not read or a staging dir not removed turns exit 0 into 1.
