## What

hv, 19 Oct 2026, at a new desk ("zeopit": two VY229HF displays above the built-in), ran Discover… and arngz refused, exit 2:

```text
arngz: the engine refused discover: the order of screen 2F1BFA83-60AB-4E33-9BC4-BE224243E845's spaces could not be read: no list in com.apple.spaces holds exactly this screen's spaces (3215), so their order is not known (request kept at .../arngz.RVlXoG/request.json); fix: fix what the engine names, then run arngz again
```

The engine orders each screen's spaces by finding the one list in com.apple.spaces' `SpacesDisplayConfiguration` that holds exactly the screen's live spaces (`opt/arngz/lib/plan.lua:618`, D22). Just after displays attach, macOS has not yet written that list. This screen had one space, so its order was never in doubt, and the refusal's fix names nothing a person can do. `apply` reads the screens the same way, so an apply at a newly attached desk is refused too.

- 04:12:58Z: `~/Library/Preferences/com.apple.spaces.plist` was last written. Read at 04:17Z through `defaults export`, it holds `[3215]` for `2F1BFA83-...` and `[3212]` for `34791BF3-...`, the two externals' spaces and the newest ids in the domain.
- 04:18:50Z: the same discover, read-only (no `--save`), through the 2.14.0 keg's arngz 1.0.1, whose `order_spaces` is identical: exit 0, with main at 11 spaces and above-left and above-right at 1 each.
- 05:05Z to 05:09Z: hv's Discover… runs 24, 26 and 27 saved zeopit, exit 0.

## Fix

1. A screen with one live space is its own order: `order_spaces` returns it without looking for a list.
2. With two or more live spaces and no list, the refusal says why and what to do: macOS has not recorded this screen's spaces yet, as just after a display attaches, so wait, then run arngz again.
3. `plan_test.lua` pins both: one space and no list is ordered, and two spaces and no list is refused with the new words.
4. `help/arngz.md` and the CHANGELOG's `[2.14.1] - unreleased` say so.

One space has one order, so nothing is guessed, and D22's rule that a screen is never ordered by guess still holds wherever there is an order to know. The comment at `:611` to `:617` says so.
2. **Two or more spaces and no list: the refusal says why and names its fix.** The not-found return (`:639` to `:642`) gains a third value, the fix. The reason becomes `com.apple.spaces has not recorded this screen's spaces (3215, 3216) yet, as just after a display attaches, so their order is not known`, and the fix is `wait for macOS to record them, then run arngz again`. The two-lists refusal (`:633` to `:635`) keeps its words and names no fix, since waiting is not known to clear it. ### Amendments

vc approved these on 19 Oct 2026, after critic-lua's read of the bytes.

9. **No desktop is added beside a lone space that no list holds.** For such a screen, `order_spaces` returns the order together with the reason and fix it gives two or more spaces with no list. observe keeps them as `order_pending` and `order_fix`. `P.plan` refuses a `create_space` on that screen (`plan.lua:398`) before anything changes, with that reason and fix. Without this, apply adds the desktop and then waits 3 s (`PHASE.created`, `arngz.lua:1149`) for a list macOS may take minutes to write. It then fails, exit 1, after a change, which breaks D25. A plan_test.lua case pins it: a desk wanting two desktops on such a screen is refused with the wait fix, and `--no-create` plans as before.
10. **order_spaces owns every order refusal's words.** observe's branch for a com.apple.spaces with no display configuration (`arngz.lua:231` to `:234`) wrote its own words, and item 1's rule did not reach it. observe now always calls `order_spaces` and passes why the plist could not be read; order_spaces gives those words for two or more spaces and orders a lone one. The test helper `discover_response.lua` answers a refusal as the engine does, with `kind = "refused"` and the fix, so its refusals reach the shim's refused branch.
11. **From the same read, within the design:**
