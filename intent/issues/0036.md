## Summary

The prez crate (`opt/prez/crate`, a workspace of prez, artifact and showreel) is hand-formatted at 2-space indent and has never been through rustfmt. `bin/.devbin/config.yaml` therefore switches `fmt rust` off (`enabled: false`). With it off, `check format` reports the Rust arm as disabled and checks nothing. The comment there records the measure: `cargo fmt --check` with `tab_spaces = 2` reported 390 hunks on 2026-09-11. hv ruled on 2026-09-14 to adopt rustfmt for the crate once ST0020 landed (hv's board, decision 1), and ST0020 closed at 5611b32.

## What devbin needs, read from its source

- `fmt rust` runs a bare `cargo fmt` (`bin/.devbin/lib/builtins:114`). This repository has no root `Cargo.toml`, because the workspace root is `opt/prez/crate`. So the arm needs a `run:` with `--manifest-path`, as `test rust` and `check clippy` already have.
- A `run:` marks the arm overridden (`bin/devbin:487`), and `check format` then refuses to derive its check from the catalogue. It dies naming the remedy: declare the check side too, as `commands.check.options.format.run` (`builtin_check_format` in `bin/.devbin/lib/cmd/check`).

## Fix

- `opt/prez/crate/rustfmt.toml` with `tab_spaces = 2`, which every crate in the workspace inherits.
- One reformat commit, `cargo fmt --all --manifest-path opt/prez/crate/Cargo.toml`, with nothing else in it.
- In `bin/.devbin/config.yaml`, `fmt rust` becomes `run: cargo fmt --all --manifest-path opt/prez/crate/Cargo.toml` and `check format` becomes `run: cargo fmt --all --check --manifest-path opt/prez/crate/Cargo.toml`. The comment is rewritten to say why both are declared.
- The check afterwards: `cargo fmt --check` is clean. cargo test, clippy with `-D warnings` and `utilz test prez` are green under bash 3.2, AT09's odd-indent check included. `bin/devbin check format` passes, and `bin/devbin fmt rust` on the formatted tree changes nothing.

CI's Rust job runs build, test and clippy, and no format check (`.github/workflows/tests.yml`). Adding one is beyond hv's ruling, so it goes to hv as a question.
