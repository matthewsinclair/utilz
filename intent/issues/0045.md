On a portrait (9:16) showreel, a socials page's handle does not shrink to fit the frame: a long handle runs off both edges. Reported by geodica on 2026-09-21, recording Snorkeltoast's reels 001 and 002 with `prez showreel video --aspect portrait` at 1080x1920 (ST0022, released in 2.11.0).

OBSERVED: the handle "forbiddenplanetnottingham" (25 characters) overflows both sides of the 1080x1920 frame. The same handle fits at 16:9. geodica worked around it in 002 by making the handle "Facebook" and putting the shop's name in the label, so nothing is blocked today.

WHY PORTRAIT ONLY: ST0022 lays a portrait frame out at half size (540 CSS px wide) and draws it at scale 2, so the player's phone layout applies. The handle's type size there is fixed rather than fitted to the width, so a long single word has nowhere to go: it cannot wrap and it does not shrink.

THE FIX, as reported: fit the socials handle to the frame's width at portrait, shrinking the type until it fits within the safe margin, never below a legible floor. geodica suggests the same for headlines in general, and that part is scope for hv. Test: a socials slide with a 25-character handle at --aspect portrait records inside the frame, and widescreen is unchanged.
