## What

`run_doctor`'s dependency sweep (`[6/7]`) reads each entry of `dependencies` and `optional_dependencies` as a map with a `name`, the form `docs/developer-guide.md:330` documents. An entry written as a bare string, as in `dependencies: [jq]`, reads `.dependencies[0].name` as empty, with status 0. So doctor checks a dependency with no name: `check_command ""` fails, the entry joins the missing list, and doctor reports `Missing dependencies:` with an empty name and counts an issue. The yaml's real fault, an entry that is not a map, is never named.

0080 checks each list's shape, not its entries'. critic-shell saw this in its read of 0080, and cc files it on vc's word (19 Oct 2026), low, for 2.14.1, after 0081.

## Fix, for the design to settle

Each entry's shape is read with its `name`. An entry that is not a map, or a map with no `name`, is refused by name, eg `beta: dependencies[0] is a string, not a map with a name`, and counted as an issue, and the sweep goes on. Whether a bare string should instead be read as a name is the design's to settle; the developer guide documents maps only.

## Measured

`yq` 4.54.1 on `dependencies: [jq]`: `.dependencies[0] | type` is `!!str`, and `.dependencies[0].name` and `.dependencies[0].app` each print nothing, with status 0. The report doctor makes from that is by reading, at `afd18e3`.
