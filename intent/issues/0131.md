## What

Twice in two days, one of CI's Rust legs stalled on the headless Chrome that prez's acceptance suites drive. On macOS, at 2.14.8's tag, prez acceptance AT21 hung with no output for more than 17 minutes, until the leg was cancelled and re-run. On ubuntu, on main's push of 1434e764, showreel AT23 and AT29 hit showreel's 30 s reply bound at frame 0 and turned main red. Today a stall turns main red or holds the post-cut CI verdict until someone re-runs the leg. Once ST0011/10's reshape fires CI's macOS legs on candidate/ (devbin-vc's message of 31 Oct), the same stall would hold a release.
