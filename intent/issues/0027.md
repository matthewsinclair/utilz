## Summary

AT09 -- "the source is standalone" -- runs four checks over `"$CRATE/src"` only (`opt/prez/crate/test/acceptance.sh:893-908`): estate paths or names, estate imports, files containing tabs, and lines at an odd indent. Since ST0017 the crate is a workspace, and `crates/artifact/src` (the shared theme, inlining and base64 code) and `crates/showreel/src` (the showreel pipeline) are compiled into the binaries prez ships, yet sit outside all four checks.
