README and help list every other reason `use brew` refuses, and not this one.

Line numbers are at `423e8b82`, whose files HEAD has not changed.

- `README.md:657` and `help/utilz.md:156` say that otherwise `use brew` "refuses, removing nothing, and names each link and the tree it serves, why no Homebrew copy would answer (...), and the remedy". A refusal because a check could not finish is not among them, so a reader who meets it has no doc that says what it means.
- The `use` usage text (`install.sh:1826-1829`) needs no change: it lists no reasons, and "`utilz use brew` removes nothing, and says why, unless Homebrew's own copy would then answer" already holds for a check that cannot finish.
