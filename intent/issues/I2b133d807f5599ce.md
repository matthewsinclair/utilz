Found by dc, and given to ic for these five READMEs by vc (hv decision 23).

## What is wrong

The License sections of `opt/expz/README.md`, `opt/macoz/README.md`, `opt/mdagg/README.md`, `opt/pdf2md/README.md` and `opt/xtrct/README.md` say "Part of Utilz framework. Personal use.", each with a copyright line of its own. Utilz's LICENSE is MIT, and so is the Homebrew formula's `license` (`packaging/homebrew/utilz.rb:16`, ST0019). A reader of one of these READMEs is told the wrong licence. Their copyright years also differ from LICENSE's 2025-2026.

## The fix

Each of the five License sections says what the root README says, with the path from the utility: "MIT. See [LICENSE](../../LICENSE)." The README's own copyright line goes, since LICENSE holds the one copy.

The same line is in the READMEs of arngz, cleanz and syncz, which are cc's, and in `opt/utilz/tmpl/README.tmpl`, which is dc's.

## The proof

Text only, so no run. After the fix, `git grep "Personal use"` finds nothing in the five files.

Resolution: Fixed at a9feb66d, which vc checked by its change: the License sections of the five READMEs say MIT and point to LICENSE. Text only, so no run.
