## Measured

- `opt/arngz/lib/arngz.lua:398` to `:408`: after `setFrame` the engine sleeps 150 ms, reads the frame back, and returns `status = "done"` only when `P.frames_agree(got, op.frame)`. ## Fix

utilz-vc's recommendation: `apply` counts a window that reached its cells' origin, at a size the app enforces, as **placed**, and says so in its report rather than silently, eg `placed at the app's own size, 375x667`. The engine already holds the readback at `arngz.lua:400`, so the test is that the origin agrees and the size does not. Reporting it matters: a window that is smaller than its cells is worth seeing, it just is not a failure.

Alternatives, each hv's to pick instead:

1. `discover` records such a window by origin only, leaving its size to the app. A desk-format change, M.
2. A never-record list naming the bundles that fix their size. XS to write, but a list to maintain, and Molt-matts 9adb5ae ("arngz: drop Appgate from every desk", 2 Nov) shows the failure mode: every later `discover` re-records it, which has already happened twice on homere and once on zeophq.

Not in this fix, each hv's to ask for separately:

- Notion's refusal in run 70 has a different cause and is filed as its own issue: `discover` recorded it clamped into the edge cell.
- `create_space` / `addSpaceToScreen returned nil` ("unable to get Mission Control data from the Dock"): 7 failed entries, confined to runs 32 and 35, both of which failed for several other reasons as well, so it decided no run's exit on its own. A watch item, not filed.
- The pointer-taken-back, full-screen-space-between-desktops, AXWebArea-title-bar and launched-with-no-window-in-time causes in runs 32, 39, 40, 44, 51, 58, 59, 60 and 64: by design or environmental on the reading so far.

With this issue, 0132 and the clamp issue all fixed, 19 of the 29 exit-1 runs in the log would have been clean: the 8 of 0132, the 10 of this one, and run 70 which needs this one and the clamp both.
